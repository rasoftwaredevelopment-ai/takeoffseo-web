# 02 — Onboarding and delivery

The checklists here are the ones in the CRM. Every item must be ticked before a job is marked done.

## SOP-05 Client onboarding

**When:** deposit or full payment received.

**Steps**
1. Send T7 (onboarding email): what you need, how to give access, what happens next, the finish date again.
2. Access, in this order. Everything stays in the client's name; you're added as a manager.
   - Google Business Profile: client adds hello@takeoffseo.com as Manager (send them the 4-step screenshot guide). If unclaimed, walk them through claiming it on a 10-minute call.
   - Website login (Fix-Up / New Website): editor or admin account created for you, not their password.
   - Google Analytics and Search Console: add as user, or you create them under the client's Google account.
   - Domain registrar (New Website only): you'll need to change DNS; get a delegate login or do it on a screen share.
   - Photos: shared folder link. Ask for 10 (Tune-Up) or 20+ (site work). Real jobs, team, vehicles, premises.
3. Send the questions sheet (T7 attachment): services, areas, jobs they want more of, jobs they don't, who they compete with, what customers ask on the phone, anything they never want said on their site.
4. Log every login in the password manager under the client's folder. Never in email, never in the CRM.
5. Everything received → tick "Onboarding complete" in the job. Clock starts. If the client's delays push past 5 business days, move the finish date by the same amount and tell them (T8 variant).

**Done when:** all access works, photos and answers received, finish date confirmed or moved in writing.

## SOP-06 Profile Tune-Up delivery ($490)

**Checklist (CRM: "Tune-Up")**
1. Baseline captured: screenshot of profile, current categories, current review count, current Insights numbers (calls, clicks, direction requests, last 28 days). This is the "before".
2. Primary category set to the one that matches the most profitable job. Secondary categories added for every other service Google has a category for.
3. Every service added, with a one-sentence description using customer words. Check against the questions sheet.
4. Service area set: the suburbs and regions they actually work. Remove any address if they're a service-area business working from home.
5. Hours set, including special hours for public holidays where known.
6. Business description written (750 characters max): what they do, where, since when if known, what makes them easy to hire. No keyword stuffing.
7. Phone, website and quote/booking links checked and matching the site exactly.
8. Photos: 10+ uploaded, named sensibly before upload (e.g. `hot-water-install-burleigh.jpg`), logo and cover set. Any stock or irrelevant photos removed.
9. Q&A: 5–8 common questions asked and answered from the owner account (from the questions sheet: "Do you do X?", "Do you charge a call-out fee?", "Which areas?").
10. Duplicate listings searched (business name, phone, address variants). Duplicates flagged for removal or merged. Record the ticket.
11. Name, address and phone made identical on 10 directories: Apple Maps, Bing Places, Yellow Pages, True Local, Yelp AU, Hotfrog, Localsearch, Facebook page, industry directory (e.g. hipages, Healthengine), plus one more relevant. Record login or claim status for each.
12. Review-request system: short Google review link created, text-message template (T-review-client) written in the client's voice, sent to client with a two-line "how to use it".
13. Tracking: Insights baseline saved; if the client has GA4, a call-click event set up on the site.
14. Quality check (SOP-16) on the profile: read every field as a customer.
15. Handover note (one page, template in `07-templates.md`) written: what changed, what to keep doing (reviews, photos monthly), recommendation for what's next, if anything.
16. 20-minute handover call booked and done.
17. Job marked done → report/handover sent (T9). Final steps: SOP-12, SOP-14.

## SOP-07 Website Fix-Up delivery ($1,900)

Everything in SOP-06, plus:

**Checklist (CRM: "Fix-Up")**
1. Baseline captured: Search Console clicks and impressions (last 3 months), GA4 enquiries if tracked, current page list, PageSpeed mobile score, screenshots of homepage on phone.
2. Competitor look: the two or three businesses appearing where the client doesn't. What pages do they have? What does their profile have? Two lines each.
3. The 20 searches that matter written down, from the free check and the questions sheet. Group by service.
4. Plan written (one page): which 5 service pages, which 2 area pages (and why those two), the technical fixes found, what the homepage will say. Sent to client (T-plan). **Client signs off before any change.** If the site turns out unfixable, stop here: SOP-19, and the $490 rule in the terms.
5. Technical fixes done: mobile layout issues, slow images compressed, broken links fixed, pages Google can't reach (check Search Console coverage), duplicate or thin pages consolidated, HTTPS working, title and description on every page, one H1 per page. Each fix logged in one line.
6. Service pages (5): each has a clear title in customer words, what you do, where, what it costs or how quoting works, 3–6 real questions answered, real photos, tap-to-call and short form. Written from the questions sheet. Client approves each page (T-approve).
7. Area pages (2): only for places with real work and real difference. Each names real suburbs served, real jobs done there, travel or call-out details. No copy-pasted text between them.
8. Homepage: first line says what and where. Services listed with links. Phone visible without scrolling on a phone.
9. Contact paths: tap-to-call on every page, short form (name, phone, what do you need) that emails the client and is tested end to end.
10. Tracking: GA4 events for call clicks, form submits, quote clicks. Search Console verified. Confirm data is arriving.
11. Google profile matched to the site: same service names, same areas, website and appointment links pointing to the right pages.
12. Quality check (SOP-16) on every page, on a phone.
13. Before-and-after report (SOP-11) written and sent (T9). Includes the "what next" note.
14. 30-minute handover call done.
15. Final invoice sent (SOP-13). Then SOP-12, SOP-14.

## SOP-08 New Website delivery ($3,900)

Everything in SOP-06, plus:

**Checklist (CRM: "New Website")**
1. Baseline captured (if replacing a site): all current URLs exported, Search Console data, which pages get traffic. If no site, note that.
2. Jobs conversation done (30–45 min call): recorded in the questions sheet. What they want more of, what they don't, how customers describe the problem, objections they hear, what makes them different in fact (not in adjectives).
3. The 20 searches that matter written down and grouped.
4. Site plan (one page): every page, its purpose, its title, what's on it. Platform chosen with client (WordPress or Squarespace unless there's a reason otherwise). Hosting option and yearly cost stated. **Client signs off.**
5. Domain and hosting: in the client's name, client's card. You're a delegate/collaborator. DNS access confirmed.
6. Build shell: platform set up, theme chosen (quiet, one accent colour, client's logo and colours), fonts readable, header with phone, footer with ABN and areas served.
7. Pages written (up to 8) from the questions sheet, in the client's words tidied. Each page: one job, clear title, real photos, questions answered, tap-to-call, form. Client approves each (T-approve). Two rounds of changes per page included; more is $150/hr, said kindly.
8. Photos: chosen, cropped, compressed, named, alt text written.
9. Forms tested end to end: submits, emails the client, thank-you message shows.
10. Tracking: GA4 with call, form and quote events; Search Console verified.
11. Redirects: every old URL mapped to a new one. Tested. No 404s for anything that had traffic.
12. Launch checklist: HTTPS, mobile, speed (PageSpeed mobile score checked and images fixed if poor), titles and descriptions, sitemap submitted, robots not blocking, favicon, 404 page, privacy page with client's ABN.
13. Google profile linked to the new pages; services and areas matched.
14. Quality check (SOP-16) on every page, on a phone, on the live domain.
15. 20-minute how-to-edit video recorded (Loom or similar): change text, swap photo, add a page, where the form emails go. Sent to client.
16. Launch report (SOP-11) sent (T9). Final invoice on go-live (SOP-13).
17. 30-day fix window: any bug or error fixed free. Task set for day 30: "Check in and close fix window."
18. Then SOP-12, SOP-14.

## SOP-09 Monthly Plan cycle ($690 Steady / $1,190 Growth)

**When:** 1st business day of the month, for each monthly client. Time budget: Steady about 4 hours a month, Growth about 8.

**Checklist (CRM: "Monthly cycle")**
1. Look at last month's numbers (calls, enquiries, direction requests, visibility for the 20 searches, reviews). Two lines: what moved, what didn't.
2. Write this month's list (T11): 3–5 items. Always includes the page(s), the profile updates, and one thing based on last month's numbers. Send. Client replies yes or changes something. No reply in 3 business days → proceed with the list.
3. Page(s): 1 (Steady) or 3 (Growth) new or improved. Same standard as SOP-07 item 6. Client approves.
4. Google profile: 2 posts (real work, plain text, a photo), new photos uploaded, new questions answered, hours checked, any Google-suggested edits reviewed.
5. Reviews: every new review gets a reply drafted and sent to the client for approval, or posted if the client has said "just post them". Review-request nudge to the client if none came in.
6. Directories: spot-check 3 of the 10 for changes. Fix anything wrong.
7. Technical: Search Console errors checked, broken links checked, site speed spot-checked. Small fixes done; bigger ones listed.
8. Growth only: competitor watch (who moved on the 20 searches, and what they changed), up to 3 profiles managed, monthly 30-minute call booked.
9. Last business day: one-page report (SOP-11, T12) sent. Growth: call held.
10. Friday updates every week the list is in progress (SOP-10).

**Done when:** report sent, every item ticked or explicitly carried over with a reason written down.

**Notice given:** finish the current month properly. Handover note (SOP-12). Never coast the last month.
