# 06 — Marketing TakeoffSEO itself

## SOP-18 Own marketing

You sell being found locally. TakeoffSEO must be findable, and the way you market must be the way you tell clients to. Nothing here costs money except time. Budget: 4 hours a week, Tuesday and Thursday mornings [confirm].

**Weekly (every week, no skipping)**
1. **TakeoffSEO's Google Business Profile.** One post: a plain-text note about something you fixed this week (no client names unless permitted), or one tip for a trade. One real photo. Check Q&A and reply to any review. Your own profile must pass your own Tune-Up checklist; run SOP-06 on it once a quarter.
2. **One useful answer in public.** A Gold Coast or Brisbane business Facebook group, a trade forum, or a reply to a local owner's question. Answer the question fully, in plain words. Don't link to the site unless asked. Your name and "TakeoffSEO, Gold Coast" is enough.
3. **One warm outreach email.** A local business you've noticed with an obvious, specific problem (profile says closed Saturday, site says open; two duplicate listings; wrong category). Three lines: what you noticed, why it costs them, free check offered. No template feel. Log it in the CRM as a lead, source "Outreach".

**Fortnightly**
4. **One partner coffee.** Web designers who don't do SEO, bookkeepers, accountants, business coaches, trade suppliers, signage companies. Ask what their clients complain about. Explain the free check and the fixed prices. Ask if you can send them a one-pager (in `07-templates.md`). Log them as a partner in the CRM. Refer work back to them whenever you can; that's the whole deal.

**Monthly**
5. **One page on takeoffseo.com.** A plain-English answer to a question owners actually ask ("Why does my competitor show up on Maps and I don't?", "Should a plumber have a page for every suburb?"). 500–800 words, written the way you'd say it, with a real screenshot. Ends with the free check. This is the site's only content plan and it's enough.
6. **Ask for permission.** Any client from the last month who was happy and hasn't been asked: SOP-14.
7. **Review your own numbers.** Where did last month's leads come from? Do more of that.

**Quarterly**
8. Run the Tune-Up checklist on your own profile. Run the free check on yourself for "SEO Gold Coast", "local SEO Brisbane", "Google Business Profile help Gold Coast" and two others. Fix what you'd fix for a client.
9. Update the website: prices if changed, new client names with permission, the monthly pages linked from the FAQ.

**Don't**
- Buy leads, run ads for yourself (until the funnel is proven and you have capacity), cold-call, mass-email, or post "5 SEO tips" content that reads like everyone else's.
- Promise anything in public you wouldn't promise on the site.
- Talk about a client's numbers, even anonymised, without permission.

**Measure:** leads by source in the monthly review (SOP-20). After three months, drop whatever hasn't produced a single lead and double whatever has.
