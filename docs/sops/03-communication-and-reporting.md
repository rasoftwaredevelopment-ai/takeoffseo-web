# 03 — Communication and reporting

## SOP-10 Friday update

**When:** every Friday, for every Active job and every monthly client with work in progress. Set aside 30 minutes at 3pm. The CRM lists who needs one.

**Steps**
1. Open the job checklist. Count what's ticked since last Friday.
2. Write T8. Four lines maximum:
   - Done this week: (items, plain words)
   - Next week: (items)
   - Need from you: (one thing, or "nothing")
   - Finish date: (unchanged, or new date and why)
3. Send. Log in the CRM ("Friday update sent").

**Rules:** never skip a Friday because "nothing happened". If nothing happened, say why and what changes. If the finish date is at risk, this is where you say it, not the week it's due (SOP-19).

## SOP-11 Reports

Three kinds. All one page. All numbers the owner cares about. No SEO scores, no "domain authority", no charts with twelve lines.

### Before-and-after report (end of Fix-Up), Launch report (end of New Website), Handover note (end of Tune-Up)

**Layout (template in `07-templates.md`)**
1. What we set out to do (two lines, from the plan).
2. What got done (the list, ticked).
3. Before and after: profile Insights (calls, clicks, directions), Search Console clicks and impressions, visibility for the 20 searches (how many you appear for now vs then), reviews. Same period lengths. If it's too early to see movement (it usually is for website changes), say so plainly and give the date to check again.
4. What to expect next (honest, two lines).
5. What next: keep going monthly ($690/$1,190), do it yourself (three things to keep doing), or nothing for now. Your recommendation and why.
6. Anything you need from them.

### Monthly report (last business day)

**Layout (T12)**
- Calls from the Google profile · Website enquiries (forms and quote clicks) · Direction requests · Visibility: appeared for X of the 20 searches (was Y) · New reviews (and average) · What I did this month · What's planned next month · Anything I need from you.
- Each number with last month beside it. One sentence at the top saying the most important thing. That's it.

**Rules:** if a number went down, say so first and say why you think. Never hide a bad month in a good chart.

## SOP-12 Handover and offboarding

**When:** a job is delivered, or a monthly client gives notice.

**Steps**
1. Handover note sent (part of the report). Includes: every login they now hold, what to keep doing monthly, the review link, where the tracking lives.
2. Access: ask whether they'd like you to stay as a manager on the profile and analytics (useful if they might come back). If not, or if they've given notice: remove yourself from Google Business Profile, GA4, Search Console, the website, the domain. Do it on a call or confirm by email so they know it happened.
3. Password manager: client folder archived. No client logins kept after offboarding.
4. CRM: stage → Delivered (then Dormant after the "what next" task) or, for monthly, → Dormant with the reason for leaving written down honestly.
5. Anything unfinished: written down and handed over. Never leave a half-built page live.

**Done when:** they have everything, you have nothing you shouldn't, and the CRM says why.

## SOP-14 Reviews, referrals and permission

**When:** 7 days after a job is delivered (the task appears), and at the end of every third monthly report.

**Steps**
1. Only ask if the work went well and the client is happy. If they're not, fix that first (SOP-19).
2. Send T10. It asks for three things, gently, in order:
   - A Google review of TakeoffSEO, with your review link. Suggest they mention what got done, not "results".
   - Permission to name them on the website ("Client: [Business name], Profile Tune-Up, [month]"). Yes/no. No pressure. The site currently says names will be added with permission; this is how that happens.
   - A referral: "If you know another owner who's invisible on Google, I'd be glad to do their free check."
3. Log the answers in the CRM (review received, permission yes/no, referral names). Permission "yes" → add the client name to the website's about page in the next site update. Nothing else: no invented quote, no result claims.
4. Thank them for whichever they did. If a referral becomes a client, thank the referrer again (a handwritten card is fine; a referral incentive is a decision for the owner, see confirm list).

**Don't:** offer anything for a review. Don't write the review for them. Don't ask twice.
