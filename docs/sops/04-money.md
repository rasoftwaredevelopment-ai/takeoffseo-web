# 04 — Money

## SOP-13 Invoicing, payments, late payment, refunds

**Prices (from the website, never changed on the phone)**
- Profile Tune-Up $490, in full before starting. Extra location $390.
- Website Fix-Up $1,900: $950 before starting, $950 when the list is done.
- New Website $3,900: $1,950 before starting, $1,950 at go-live.
- Monthly Steady $690 / Growth $1,190: start of each month, in advance.
- Extra work $150/hr, estimated in writing first, invoiced with the next invoice.
- All plus GST [confirm registration]. Australian dollars.

**Invoicing**
1. Invoices come from the accounting tool [confirm: Xero / MYOB], from Alexander Nott trading as TakeoffSEO, ABN [ABN]. Numbered. Due in 7 days. **Bank transfer only.** Account name, BSB and account number on every invoice, and the invoice number as the reference. No card payments, no payment links.
2. Every invoice names the package and the finish date (one-off) or the month (monthly). One line, no jargon.
3. Deposit invoice goes with the booking confirmation (T6). Final invoice goes with the report (T9). Monthly invoice goes on the 1st with the month's list (T11).
4. Record every invoice in the CRM against the job: sent date, due date, paid date.

**Payment received**
- Check the bank feed each morning (two minutes) and mark the invoice paid in the CRM the same day. The stage change and onboarding email depend on it.
- Deposit or full → stage Active, onboarding starts (SOP-05). Same day.
- Final → job closed. Thank them in the review email (T10).
- Monthly → tick in the CRM. Nothing to send.

**Late payment**
- Day 7 (due date): nothing. People are busy.
- Day 10: T14, friendly, one line, invoice attached again.
- Day 17: short call or text. "Did the invoice get lost?" Ask if there's a problem with the work; if there is, that's SOP-19, not a money problem.
- Day 21 (monthly): pause work, say so kindly, resume when paid. (One-off, final invoice): the work is already delivered; keep asking politely every two weeks. No threats. Small claims is the last resort and rarely worth it at these amounts; decide case by case.
- Never withhold access to the client's own accounts over a bill. They own them.

**The finish-date rule**
- If the finish date is missed for reasons on your side, the final invoice isn't sent until the list is done. Say so in the Friday update so they hear it from you.
- If the delay is the client's (access, photos, answers), the date moves by the same amount, in writing (T8 variant). The invoice timing doesn't change.

**Refunds**
- Before work starts: full refund, no questions, within 5 business days.
- Fix-Up found unfixable at plan stage: client pays $490 (plan + Tune-Up), keeps both, remaining deposit refunded. $490 credited to a New Website if booked within 3 months.
- After work starts: no refunds for change of mind; the list gets finished. If you've genuinely done a bad job, fix it free first; if it still can't be fixed, refund what's fair and write down why. Australian Consumer Law applies and the terms say so.
- Monthly: no refund for the current month; 30 days' notice; finish the month properly.

**Bookkeeping**
- Weekly (Friday, SOP-20): reconcile payments, chase anything past day 10.
- Monthly: set aside GST and tax [confirm % with accountant]. Software subscriptions reviewed quarterly.
- Keep it boring. Boring money is good money.
